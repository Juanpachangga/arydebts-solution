# Usability 217.24

Premium Home status/date controls are centered in their content area, with equal-width status and calendar cells. The Home-only date shortcut and date behavior remain unchanged.

The contribution simulator explains all four fields in Spanish, English and Portuguese. A worked example uses 100 initially plus 50 at each month end for one year at 0%, yielding 700 in personal contributions. Trying the example only changes the unsaved scenario. The result distinguishes the final amount, personal contributions and calculated return change. Unknown or blank return can be zero; nonzero rates are explicitly assumptions. An expandable calculation explanation documents the fixed nominal annual rate divided by 12, end-of-month contributions and omitted taxes, fees and inflation.

The installation planet and text now share flex alignment inside preferences, where the former selector only applied to main content. The installation guide image cannot shrink and stays centered against its text block.

Checks: Premium center/no-overlap geometry through 320–1920px, three locales and both themes; simulator example, zero/positive/negative return, accessible field hints, responsive layout, draft preservation and unchanged account storage; preferences planet/text alignment at 320/390/1366px and three locales. Browser engines: Chromium and WebKit.
